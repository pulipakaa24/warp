Metal: ``fast_math`` modules reproduce CUDA's ``--use_fast_math`` results bit for bit by default (``warp.config.metal_fast_math = "cuda"``): float32 division is ``div.approx.ftz.f32`` (``MUFU.RCP`` + ``FMUL.FTZ``) and ``sqrt`` is ``sqrt.approx.ftz.f32`` (``MUFU.SQRT``), emulated exactly in integer arithmetic. ``"relaxed"`` keeps Metal's own fast division and square root, ``"off"`` ignores ``fast_math``.
